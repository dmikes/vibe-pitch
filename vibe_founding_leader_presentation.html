VIBE Academy Founding School Leader Presentation

Constellation Precision • Personalized Student Pathways

A Coordinated Launch: Precise Systems meet Personalized Flight. My passion for aerospace engineering, background in cybersecurity, and work with Vibe coding drives my approach to launching VIBE Academy. Just like a satellite constellation deploying in tight formation while making micro-adjustments to stay in orbit, a successful startup needs airtight operations that adapt to individual needs. Previously, as an educational consultant, I built a custom, personalized app that allowed students at another startup school to design their own learning paths. That system used an intake profile to automatically generate applied STEM, CTE, and entrepreneurial projects mapped to local state standards—allowing every student to carve out their own trajectory while seamlessly tallying their credits. This interactive dashboard showcases that same AI-driven, student-centered mission in action.
